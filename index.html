About Me

Curiosity, with a scientific temperament.

I am a versatile generalist with a curiosity-driven approach and cross-functional experience spanning intellectual property, technology transfer, innovation, and business development.

With a Master's degree in Biochemistry, I have a strong foundation in the life sciences and a natural interest in how science and technology translate into real-world applications — spanning healthcare, biotechnology, and emerging technologies.

I have worked with researchers, startups, academic institutions, industry partners, MSMEs, and incubators across the innovation ecosystem — on IP assessment and analytics, prior-art and patentability searches, market and competition analysis, technology transfer, licensing, and business development.

More recently, my interest has grown toward AI, AI security, and AI governance — how these systems are built, secured, and regulated — as well as biosecurity, where my biochemistry background meets questions of safety and risk in the life sciences.

At the heart of it, I enjoy figuring things out — especially problems that sit between disciplines and don't have an obvious, predefined answer.
